The Stage Is Set

Bengaluru serves as the perfect backdrop for this historic transition, representing the fusion of tradition and digital transformation. In the heart of India’s Silicon Valley, we aren’t just witnessing a change in leadership—we are witnessing an evolution.

As we stand at the threshold of a new era, ANANTA 2026 brings together the brightest minds of the RSA MDIO. This 17th Installation Ceremony is more than just a formal event; it is a celebration of leadership, service, and the infinite potential of Rotaract.

A Visionary Handover

The legacy of RSA MDIO takes a bold leap forward as we officially install our incoming leader:

Rtn. Rtr. Arun Teja Godavarthi

President, RY 2026-27, RSAMDIO

Taking over the office with a mandate for innovation, Rtr. Arun Teja is committed to fostering a culture of excellence. His vision ensures that every participant leaves Bengaluru inspired to create lasting, "limitless" change in their communities.

The ROAR Awards: A Night of Excellence

The celebration of impact doesn’t end with the ceremony. On the same night, we transition from the prestige of the Installation to the high-energy pulse of the ROAR Awards.

This is our moment to recognize the fiercest "lions" of the movement. We will honor the outstanding projects, tireless service, and the remarkable milestones achieved by Rotaractors over the past year. It’s a night of glitz, gratitude, and gold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about dates, venue, registration, and who hosts ANANTA 2026.

What is ANANTA 2026?

ANANTA 2026 is the 17th Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSA MDIO) Installation Ceremony, held together with the ROAR Awards in Bengaluru. It celebrates the handover of leadership and impact across the Rotaract South Asia footprint.

When and where is it?

May 30–31, 2026, primarily at Ramada by Wyndham Bengaluru Yelahanka, Doddaballapur Road, Yelahanka, Bengaluru 560064, Karnataka, India. The on-page schedule lists day-of timings.

How do I register?

Use the official link go.rsamdio.org/ananta — the same destination as every Register button on this site.

Who organizes the event?

Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSA MDIO) is the organizing body. The main site is rsamdio.org.
